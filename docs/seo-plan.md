# Second Query SEO plan

This is the working plan for organic search. The weekly SEO task reads it at the start of every run, works through it, and adds a line to the log at the bottom. Sam can edit any of it.

## Goal

Rank as high as possible in Google and Bing (UK) for generic searches by UK businesses looking for independent Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising help, and turn that traffic into briefs. Pages win by being the most useful, specific and trustworthy answer, not by repeating keywords.

## Rules

- Every page must be genuinely useful on its own and clearly different from every other page. No doorway pages, no near-duplicate city or industry pages, no keyword stuffing, no hidden text.
- Never invent anything: no testimonials, reviews, ratings, client names, logos, results, statistics, awards, certifications or years of experience that Sam hasn't confirmed. No `AggregateRating` or `Review` schema.
- Never mention or hint at Sam's employer, its clients or any account he works on.
- Facts about Google Ads or Microsoft Advertising must come from a source read in that run, ideally official help pages. Paraphrase; at most one quote under 15 words per source.
- Don't change prices, service terms, the Account X-ray logic or the games. The monthly best-practice task owns the X-ray rules.
- UK English, plain and direct, no em dashes. Match the voice of the existing pages.
- One primary query per page (see the map). If a new page would compete with an existing one, improve the existing page instead.
- No link buying, link exchanges or automated outreach. Off-site ideas go to Sam as suggestions.
