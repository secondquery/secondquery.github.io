Google's release notes for Google Ads Editor 2.14 include one line that's easy to miss: AI Max, text asset automation and Final URL expansion are now activated by default when you create new Search campaigns.

If your team builds campaigns in Editor, which most larger accounts do, every new Search campaign will now arrive with AI Max switched on unless someone changes it before posting.

## Why this matters

AI Max isn't a small setting. It lets Google match your ads to searches beyond your keywords, rewrite parts of your ad text and send people to whichever page on your site it thinks fits best. Those are three separate decisions about reach, messaging and landing pages, made in one click.

Sometimes that's exactly right. Google's own data, published at launch, says advertisers who activate AI Max typically see 14% more conversions or conversion value at a similar cost per acquisition or return on ad spend, and more for campaigns built mostly on exact and phrase match.

Independent results have been more mixed. PPC Land's round-up of AI Max cites a Smarter Ecommerce analysis of more than 250 retail campaigns that found AI Max conversions delivered about 35% lower return on ad spend than other match types. Results like that don't mean AI Max is bad. They mean it depends heavily on the account, the goals and the data feeding it.

## Make it a decision, not a default

The risk with defaults is that nobody decides. Six months later, half the account is running AI Max because of how it was built, not because anyone chose it.

Three things I'd put in place:

1. **A build checklist.** Add "AI Max: on or off, and why" to whatever checklist your team or agency uses before posting from Editor.
2. **A test, not a rollout.** If you want AI Max, switch it on through a proper experiment on a few campaigns first, with a clear success measure agreed in advance.
3. **Guardrails before reach.** If you use Final URL expansion, check which pages Google can send people to and exclude any that shouldn't receive paid traffic. Review text customisation against your brand and compliance rules.

## The question for leadership

If you're responsible for a Google Ads budget but don't build campaigns yourself, the simple question for your team or agency is: "Which of our Search campaigns use AI Max, and was that a decision?"

If the answer takes a while, that's worth knowing.
