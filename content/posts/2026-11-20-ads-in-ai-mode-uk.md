On 28 October, Google said it was scaling ads in AI Mode to ten more countries, and that it plans to add the United Kingdom, Brazil and Mexico in the fourth quarter of 2026. No exact date has been given for the UK, so treat it as "any week now" rather than a fixed launch.

AI Mode itself isn't new here. It has been available to UK users since July 2025. What's changing is that sponsored results will start appearing inside it.

## There's no sign-up, and no switch

The most common question I hear is "how do we get in early?" The honest answer is that you can't, and you don't need to.

According to Google's help page, existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to serve in AI Overviews and AI Mode. There are no new campaigns or separate budgets. You also can't target AI Mode on its own, or opt out of it separately from Search.

So the useful question isn't "how do we get in?" It's "what will our current set-up do when this goes live?"

## How your keywords will be matched

This is the part that matters most for budget decisions. Google describes two ways an ad can be matched in AI Mode:

- **Directly to the query**, as in normal search.
- **Contextually**, to the query and the AI response together.

AI-powered targeting (AI Max, broad match, Shopping and Performance Max) is eligible for both. Exact and phrase match keywords are only eligible when the query itself shows direct commercial intent, not for broader, exploratory questions.

In plain terms: the more of your account that runs on broad match, AI Max or Performance Max, the more of your spend could flow into these longer, more conversational searches. Google says AI Mode queries are, on average, three times longer than traditional searches, based on US data.

## You won't see it separately in reporting

Google's help page says performance from AI Overviews and AI Mode is included in aggregate in your standard campaign reports, and the triggering queries appear in aggregate in the search terms report. There's no separate AI Mode segment.

That means a shift in performance could show up with no obvious cause in the account. If your cost per acquisition moves in December, AI Mode is one possible reason you won't be able to isolate cleanly.

## Five things worth doing before it lands

1. **Know your mix.** Work out what share of spend runs through broad match, AI Max and Performance Max. That's your exposure to contextual matching.
2. **Tidy your negatives and brand exclusions.** Google says they still apply in AI Mode, and they're your main way to keep ads away from queries you don't want.
3. **Write down a baseline.** Note cost per acquisition or return on ad spend, conversion rate and search term mix for the last four weeks, by campaign type. You'll want it when someone asks what changed.
4. **Check what you're optimising towards.** Contextual matching leans heavily on Smart Bidding. If your conversion values or primary conversions are off, AI Mode will scale the problem.
5. **Agree who's watching.** Decide now whether your team or your agency owns the weekly check on search terms once ads go live.

## The bottom line

This isn't a reason to rebuild your account. It's a reason to make sure the account you already have would make good decisions in a new kind of search, because it's going to be making them whether you act or not.
