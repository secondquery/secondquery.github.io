People also ask

The questions that usually come up on the first call.

Do you need access to our Google Ads account?
For a Decision Review, yes: read-only access, or minimised exports if you prefer. I never make changes, and nothing is shared with anyone else. A consultation can work from screenshots or a short export.
Will this undermine our agency or our in-house team?
It shouldn't, and that's deliberate. I don't pitch for management work, so I have no reason to talk down the people running your account. Reviews are written to help them succeed, and many clients share them with their agency.
How is this different from a free audit?
Free audits are usually a sales tool for account management. A Decision Review is paid for by you, answers your question and is written by someone who won't be selling you anything afterwards.
What size of business is this for?
It fits best for UK companies spending roughly £25,000 to £100,000 a month on Google Ads. Larger accounts are welcome and quoted separately; smaller ones often get more from a consultation.
Do you work on Microsoft Advertising too?
Yes. Most Reviews look at Google Ads first, but Microsoft Advertising is part of the picture wherever it carries meaningful spend or opportunity.
