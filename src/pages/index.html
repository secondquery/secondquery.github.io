Decision Review

Prepared for your leadership team

1. What I'd prioritise

The current set-up is broadly sound, so the biggest gains look to be in measurement. I'd suggest fixing lead values first, then testing a move of around a third of non-brand budget from Performance Max into two tightly themed Search campaigns.

2. Evidence from your account

41% of reported conversions are brand searches. Three campaigns are capped by budget at a cost per lead 22% under target. Offline sales aren't feeding bidding.

3. Risks and what would change my view

Lead quality could dip for two to three weeks while bidding recalibrates. If CRM-matched sales fall more than 10%, it would be worth pausing and reverting.

4. A suggested 90-day plan

Weeks 1 to 2: lead values and offline import. Weeks 3 to 6: the budget move. Weeks 7 to 12: a clean AI Max test on one campaign.

People also ask

The questions that usually come up on the first call.

Do you need access to our Google Ads account?
For a Decision Review, yes: read-only access, or minimised exports if you prefer. I never make changes, and nothing is shared with anyone else. A consultation can work from screenshots or a short export.
Will this undermine our agency or our in-house team?
It shouldn't, and that's deliberate. I don't pitch for management work, so I have no reason to talk down the people running your account. Reviews are written to help them succeed, and many clients share them with their agency.
How is this different from a free audit?
Free audits are usually a sales tool for account management. A Decision Review is paid for by you, answers your question and is written by someone who won't be selling you anything afterwards. More on independent Google Ads audits.
What size of business is this for?
All sizes. Decision Reviews and advisory suit UK companies spending roughly £25,000 to £100,000 a month. Smaller businesses usually start with a £295 consultation or a £795 Focused Review, and larger accounts are quoted separately.
Do you work with in-house PPC teams?
Yes. In-house teams can book an expert session, a second opinion on a plan, a team workshop or weekly advisory. I don't manage accounts, so I'm not angling to take yours over. See the options for PPC teams.
Do you work on Microsoft Advertising too?
Yes. Most Reviews look at Google Ads first, but Microsoft Advertising is part of the picture wherever it carries meaningful spend or opportunity.
