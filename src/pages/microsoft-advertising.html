What a Microsoft review looks at

The same questions as a Google review, judged against how Microsoft actually works. Every finding is tied to what it means for your budget, your targets or the decision you're facing.

  • The Google import. Microsoft itself recommends reviewing bids, budgets and settings after an import. Scheduled imports also keep copying changes across from Google, which can quietly undo edits made in Microsoft.
  • Where the ads appear. Bing, Microsoft's search partners and any Audience Network placements, and what each one is worth to you.
  • LinkedIn profile targeting. In UK Search campaigns, company, industry and job function data from LinkedIn works as a bid adjustment: it raises or lowers bids for the people you care about rather than narrowing who sees the ads.
  • Measurement through UET. Whether the UET tag and conversion goals are set up, and whether consent mode is handled, which decides how many UK conversions Microsoft can see.
  • Search terms and negatives. Negatives copied from Google often leave gaps, so the search terms report is checked on Microsoft's own data.
  • The budget split. Whether Microsoft deserves more, less or the same share of spend, based on cost per lead or return rather than habit.

People also ask

Is Microsoft Advertising worth it for a B2B business?
It depends on your market and margins. Microsoft reaches searchers who aren't on Google, and LinkedIn profile data can sharpen B2B bidding, but volumes are usually smaller. A review sets the budget split from your own numbers rather than a rule of thumb.
Can't we just import our Google Ads campaigns?
Importing is a sensible start, and Microsoft makes it easy. The problems come afterwards: settings that suit Google but not Microsoft, scheduled imports overwriting changes, and conversion tracking that was never set up on the Microsoft side.
Do you need access to our Microsoft account?
A read-only user invite is easiest, but exports work too. I never make changes, and access is removed at the end.
Will you run our Microsoft Advertising for us?
No. Second Query is advice only. Your team or agency keeps running the account, and the findings are written so they can act on them straight away.
