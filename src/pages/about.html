Hi, I'm Sam

I've spent my whole career in paid search, agency-side, and I now lead search for large, multi-market advertisers. Second Query is how I help businesses outside that world, without managing their accounts.

Most of my work has been on the kind of accounts where small decisions move big numbers: lots of markets, lots of products and a lot of automation to keep honest. Along the way I've built governance frameworks, measurement set-ups and bidding tests, and I've sat on both sides of agency reviews.

What I noticed is that the most expensive mistakes rarely come from a bad setting. They come from a big decision made without a clear, independent read: an agency fired for problems that were really about tracking, a budget doubled into campaigns that had already run out of good demand, or a new Head of Performance spending their first quarter just working out what's going on.

Second Query exists for those moments. It's deliberately small: one senior person, a fixed price, and advice whose only job is to be right.

The breadth helps. I've run search for quick-service restaurants, fashion and high-street retailers, spirits brands, luxury hotels, an insurer, estate agents, a housebuilder, a housing association and a wine merchant. As a business director I led 13 multi-market accounts at once, and earlier ran paid search across 12 European markets.

Why "Second Query"?

When the first search doesn't quite answer the question, people search again, more precisely. That second query is usually where the real intent shows up.

The same is true of big decisions. The first answer often comes from whoever is closest to the account. A second, independent one tends to be where the useful detail is.
