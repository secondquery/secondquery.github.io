Account X-ray

A free first look at your Google Ads or Microsoft Advertising account. Drop in a report you can export in a minute and get an instant read on the things that usually matter most. Your file is read by your own browser and never uploaded anywhere.

1

Export two reports

The X-ray reads two standard reports. Use the last 90 days if you can (30 at the very least), so there's enough data for the results to mean something, and leave segments off so each campaign or search term is one row.

Report 1: Campaignsabout 2 minutes
  1. In Google Ads, go to Campaigns. If you can't see it, type "Campaigns" into the search bar at the top.
  2. Set the date range (top right) to the last 90 days.
  3. Show campaigns that spent in that period, including paused ones. Leave removed campaigns out.
  4. Click the Columns icon and make sure these are ticked. The impression share columns sit under Competitive metrics.
CampaignCostConversionsConv. valueCampaign typeBid strategy typeClicksSearch lost IS (budget)Search lost IS (rank)
  1. Click Download (the down-arrow icon above the table) and choose .csv.
Report 2: Search termsabout 2 minutes
  1. Go to Insights and reports, then Search terms (or type "Search terms" into the search bar at the top).
  2. Use the same 90-day date range, with all campaigns selected.
  3. Check these columns are showing:
Search termCostConversionsMatch typeConv. valueClicksCampaign
  1. Click Download and choose .csv. Performance Max search terms appear in this report too.
Report 1: Campaignsabout 2 minutes
  1. In Microsoft Advertising, open the Campaigns page.
  2. Set the date range to the last 90 days.
  3. Click Columns, then Modify columns, and add these. Impression share columns are under Competitive metrics.
Campaign nameSpendConversionsRevenueCampaign typeBid strategy typeClicksImpr. share lost to budget (%)Impr. share lost to rank (%)
  1. Click Download and choose CSV.
Report 2: Search termsabout 3 minutes
  1. Go to Reports and create a Search term report.
  2. Use the same 90-day date range and all campaigns.
  3. Include these columns, then run it and download as CSV:
Search termSpendConversionsDelivered match typeRevenueClicksCampaign name

Pink columns are needed. The rest make the read much sharper, and the X-ray tells you which ones it found.

The platform guidance behind the X-ray

Reviewed against Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising guidance on 20 November 2026, and checked every month. The X-ray only sees what's in your file: it can't see margins, lead quality, attribution or the auction, so observations are prompts to look closer, not conclusions.

    The learning clock

    Changed a bid strategy, target or budget? Google's "Learning" label clears within seven days, but its guidance says calibration can take up to around 50 conversions or three conversion cycles. Put in your numbers to see how long to wait before judging the change.
