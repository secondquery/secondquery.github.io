Account X-ray

A free first look at your Google Ads or Microsoft Advertising account. Drop in a report you can export in a minute and get an instant read on the things that usually matter most. Your file is read by your own browser and never uploaded anywhere.

1

Export a report

Campaign report: open the Campaigns table, pick the last 30 or 90 days, make sure Cost, Conversions and Conv. value are showing (add Search lost IS (budget) if you can), then download it as a CSV.

Search terms report: open the Search terms report for the same dates and download it as a CSV.

Campaign report: on the Campaigns tab, pick your date range, show Spend, Conversions and Revenue (add Lost impression share (budget) if you can), then download as CSV.

Search terms report: from Reports, run a Search term report for the same dates and download as CSV.

2

Tell it your brand

Comma-separated. Used to split brand from non-brand. Campaigns with "brand" in the name count too.

3

Drop in one or both files

Your file is read by your browser and never leaves this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent. Close the tab and it's gone.

Where I'd look first

    The X-ray only sees what's in the file. It can't see your margins, lead quality, attribution or what's happening in the auction, which is usually where the biggest answers are. That's what a Decision Review is for.

    The learning clock

    Changed a bid strategy, target or budget? Google's "Learning" label clears within seven days, but its guidance says calibration can take up to around 50 conversions or three conversion cycles. Put in your numbers to see how long to wait before judging the change.

    Read more in the note on Smart Bidding learning.
