    Paid Search Decision Review

    £1,750Fixed fee · ten working days

    For a decision with real money attached: a new Head of Performance starting, an agency change, bringing PPC in-house or a budget step-up.

    Start a brief

    What it answers

    One question, agreed on the first call. For example: "What should our new Head of Performance fix first?", "Is our agency the problem, or is it our set-up?" or "Can Google Ads take another £20,000 a month, and where?"

    What I look at

    • Measurement. Conversion actions, values, consent mode and enhanced conversions, and whether bidding is optimising towards the right thing.
    • Economics. Targets against margin and lifetime value, and whether ROAS or CPA goals reflect what the business actually needs.
    • Bidding and automation. Smart Bidding, Performance Max, AI Max for Search and where automation is helping or hiding things.
    • Budget and headroom. Where spend is capped, where returns are flattening and where the next pound is likely to work hardest.
    • Queries and brand. What you're really paying for, including how much of the result is brand demand you'd win anyway.
    • Structure and ways of working. Whether the account set-up and the team or agency process support the decision you're making.

    What you get

    • A written Review: the recommendation first, then the evidence, the risks and a 90-day plan.
    • A 90-minute readout with your team, and your agency if you'd like them there.
    • Two weeks of follow-up questions by email after the readout.

    Best fit: UK businesses spending roughly £25,000 to £100,000 a month. Larger or multi-market accounts are welcome and quoted separately.
